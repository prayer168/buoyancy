【English】
🧪🌊 Big update to Buoyancy Explorer — three hands-on experiments just landed in the Lab tab!

Built with AI pair-programming, this free interactive lesson for upper elementary now pairs on-screen simulations with real tabletop experiments: find the success condition on screen first, then prove it with your own hands.

📌 Clay Boat Challenge: the same 300 g of clay sinks as a ball but floats as a bowl — then see how many coins it can carry
📌 Cartesian Diver: drag a slider to squeeze the bottle and watch the air bubble shrink as the diver sinks
📌 DIY Submarine: flood or empty the ballast tank to dive, hover, and surface like the real thing
📌 Every experiment ships with a materials list, build steps, observation prompts, and safety notes

The best part — kids first discover "how big a bowl do I need?" in the simulation, then grab the clay and verify it: predict, test, revise, just like real scientists.

AI isn't here to replace teachers. It takes the repetitive, tedious parts of prep off our plate, so we can spend our time on what truly matters: being there for our kids. ❤️
