【繁體中文】
「重的東西一定會沉嗎？」答案不只和重量有關，還藏在體積、液體密度與排開的水裡。

《浮力探險所》是一份適合國小高年級的互動數位教材。孩子可以調整物體質量、體積與液體種類，先預測再測試，並透過分段動畫、生活應用翻牌與十題闖關，用證據理解漂浮、懸浮和下沉。

教材支援電腦、平板與手機，歡迎點進去動手找出答案：

【English】
“Do heavy objects always sink?” The answer depends on more than weight—it also involves volume, fluid density, and the amount of fluid displaced.

Buoyancy Explorer is an interactive science lesson for upper elementary students. Learners can adjust an object’s mass, volume, and surrounding fluid, make a prediction, and test it. Step-by-step animations, real-life application cards, and a ten-question challenge help them explain floating, neutral buoyancy, and sinking with evidence.

The lesson works on computers, tablets, and phones. Explore it here:
https://prayer168.github.io/buoyancy/
